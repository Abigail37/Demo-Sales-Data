# Sales Test Demo Report 📊
A Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and regional trends using a demo retail sales dataset.

## Project Overview
This project provides a business intelligence view of sales operations across products, customers, regions, and payment methods. The dashboard was designed to help stakeholders track revenue growth, identify high-performing products, monitor regional profitability, and understand customer purchasing patterns.

## Problem Statement
The business needed a centralized dashboard to answer key questions such as:

* Which products generate the highest profit?
* Which regions contribute the most revenue?
* How do monthly sales trends fluctuate?
* Which product categories perform best?
* What customer and payment insights can support business decisions?

# Key Dashboard Visuals
## KPI Cards
The dashboard highlights:
* Total Sales Revenue
* Total Profit
* Total Quantity Sold
* Completed Orders
These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

## Monthly Sales Trend
A line chart visualizes quantity sold across months.

### Key Observation:
* Sales peaked around July
* October recorded the lowest sales activity
* Performance fluctuated throughout the year, showing seasonal demand patterns

## Customer Insights
A customer table highlights top customers based on total purchase value.

### Key Observation:
* Julie Murray generated the highest customer sales value
* Several customers contributed consistently high transaction amounts

## Product Insights
A detailed product table tracks: Invoice IDs, Product names, Sales revenue, and Profit generated per transaction. This helps identify high-value product transactions.

## Profit by Product Category
A donut chart compares profitability across categories:
* Electronics generated the highest profit contribution

## Regional Profit Analysis
A funnel chart compares profit generated across regions.

### Key Observation:

## Profit by Product

### Top Performing Products:

# Key Insights
### Electronics Drive Business Growth
Electronics contributed more than half of total profits, making it the strongest-performing product category.

**Recommendation:**
Increase inventory investment and promotional focus on electronics products.

### Strong Regional Dependence
The South and West regions generated the highest profits.

**Recommendation:**
Investigate opportunities to improve sales penetration in the East region.

### Seasonal Sales Fluctuation
Sales activity peaked mid-year and dropped significantly in October.

**Recommendation:**
Introduce targeted campaigns during low-performing months to stabilize sales performance.

### Product Profitability Gap
Phones and laptops significantly outperformed accessories and peripherals.

**Recommendation:**
Bundle lower-performing products with high-performing devices to improve overall sales volume.
